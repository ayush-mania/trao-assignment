How we interview engineers

Our interview process for backend and platform roles has four stages:

  1. A 30 minute recruiter screen.
  2. A take-home exercise: build a small service that ingests robot telemetry. You have four days; we care about tests and error handling more than features.
  3. A system design interview (60 minutes) about scaling event pipelines and fleet coordination.
  4. A behavioural conversation with two engineers about ownership, mentoring and incidents you have handled.

We do not do whiteboard algorithm puzzles.
